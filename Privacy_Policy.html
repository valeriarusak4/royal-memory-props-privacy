Royal: Memory Props

Privacy Policy

Effective date and last updated: October 20, 2026

This policy explains how the developer of Royal: Memory Props handles information when you use the iOS app, contact support or visit this policy page.

Your game progress and personal poster are processed and saved on your device. The app does not upload them to the developer. It has no account registration, advertising SDKs or third-party analytics SDKs. Optional support emails and visits to this website are described separately below.

1. Information handled on your device

The app stores the following information in its local app storage to provide its features:

  • Game progress: completed levels, scores, stars, best times, unlocked collectible props, tutorial status and the current attempt needed to resume a show.
  • Preferences: music, sound effects, haptics and reduced-motion settings.
  • Optional personal poster: a processed copy of the photo you choose or take, together with your caption, selected poster style and decorative props.

This information is used to save your progress, remember your preferences and create your personal poster. The app does not send it to the developer or to an app-operated server. The developer cannot view your locally stored photos, poster or game saves.

2. Photos, camera and optional permissions

Creating a personal poster is optional. You can play the game without providing a photo or granting camera access.

  • Photo selection: the app uses Apple's system photo picker and receives the image you select, rather than browsing your entire photo library.
  • Camera: camera permission is requested when you choose the Camera option to take a photo for your poster.
  • Photo processing: resizing and cropping take place on your device. The app does not perform facial recognition or biometric identification.

You can change camera permission in iOS Settings. Revoking permission prevents future camera access; it does not automatically delete a photo already saved in your personal poster. You can delete that poster using the controls described below.

3. Advertising, tracking and third-party SDKs

The app does not include advertising networks, third-party analytics SDKs or tracking SDKs. It does not access your advertising identifier, track you across other companies' apps or websites, sell your information or share your local game and poster data with advertisers.

Apple may separately process information for the App Store, operating-system services or diagnostics according to your Apple privacy settings and Apple's Privacy Policy .

4. Storage, retention and deletion

Local data remains in the app's storage until you change it, reset it, delete the relevant content or delete the app.

  • Delete your poster: use the delete control in the personal poster editor to remove the saved poster photo and its associated poster information from the app.
  • Reset game progress: use the reset controls in the app's Settings. If a poster exists, you can choose whether to keep it or delete it with the game progress. Resetting game progress preserves your preferences.
  • Remove local app storage: use Delete App in iOS. Offloading the app is different and may retain its documents and data.

Deleting a poster does not delete the original photo from your photo library. Any copies you keep outside the app must be managed separately.

Depending on your device and backup settings, iOS may include app data in device backups. The app does not operate its own cloud account or cloud-sync service. Manage device backups through your Apple settings; deleting local content does not necessarily remove existing backup copies.

5. Information you send to support

If you voluntarily email valeriarusak4@gmail.com , the developer receives your email address and any message, attachments or other information you choose to include. This information is used to answer your request and resolve the issue you report, not for advertising.

Support correspondence is retained only for as long as needed to handle your inquiry and meet applicable legal obligations. You may request deletion of support correspondence by emailing the same address. Email delivery and storage also involve your email provider and the recipient's email provider under their own privacy practices.

Please send only information needed to explain your request. The developer cannot remotely retrieve or erase data stored solely inside the app on your device.

6. Hosting of this policy page

This page is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub states that it logs and stores visitors' IP addresses for security purposes, including visits made without signing in. This hosting activity is separate from the app's local processing of game data and photos.

This page does not add analytics scripts, advertising trackers, contact forms or embedded third-party content. For GitHub's handling of hosting information, see GitHub Pages data collection and the GitHub General Privacy Statement .

7. Children's privacy

The app does not require an account or ask users to provide their age, contact details or a public profile. Its local game and poster processing applies regardless of the user's age. Parents and guardians can manage camera permissions, photos and app storage through the device settings and the controls described in this policy.

If you have a concern about information a child has sent to support, please contact the developer.

8. Your choices and privacy requests

You can choose not to use the photo feature, change available permissions, delete a poster or reset local progress. For questions or requests concerning information held by the developer, such as support correspondence, use the contact address below. Applicable privacy rights depend on your location and the information involved; requests will be handled in accordance with applicable law.

9. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated to reflect changes to the app or its information handling. Any updated policy will be published on this page with a revised effective date. If a future feature requires additional permissions or consent, those will be requested as required.

10. Contact

For app support or privacy questions about Royal: Memory Props, contact the developer at valeriarusak4@gmail.com .
